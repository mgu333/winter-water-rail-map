Sources, timing & limitations

Walking, cycling and driving are calculated separately with Valhalla over OpenStreetMap. Times exclude parking, traffic and activity duration. The 15-minute walk and 30-minute bike limits are one way. Scuba equipment may make driving preferable.

Rail geometry: US DOT / FRA / BTS NTAD Amtrak Routes, updated May 2026. This is a mapped railway alignment, not a guarantee of future operations. Schedule and fares need a date-specific check.

Activity pins are site reference points; check the final entrance and availability. Some ocean activities share a launch site. Point Lobos diving requires booking; fishing restrictions and closures apply.

The inherited 2070 snowfall layer is an illustrative screening scenario, not a validated local forecast. It does not establish future livability.
